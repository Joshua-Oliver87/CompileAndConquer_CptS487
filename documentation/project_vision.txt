Controls:
The player is moved with the Arrow Keys
Z causes a short barrage of shots to be fired; it can be held down for rapid fire
X releases a bomb (if the player has any left)
Shift slows the character’s movements
Esc pauses the fame and displays an in-game menu
Q returns you to the title screen when the game is paused
R returns you to the beginning of stage 1 when the game is paused

Player Stats:
Player has 100 Hp
Player has 3 lives
Player has 2 bombs
The bomb attack does heavy damage to enemies it touches, and gets rid of any bullets in its way

Boss Stats:
Mid boss has 100 Hp
Final boss has 200 Hp

Scoring: 
Hitting an enemy gives 500 points
Defeating an enemy gives you 10,000 points
Collecting items gives you 10,000 points
Grazing bullets gives you 1000 points
Each life remaining gives you 100,000 points.

Gameplay: 
Regular play with grunts - 30 sec
4 grunts move across screen from left to right
4 grunts move across screen from right to left
Above pattern is repeated.
Fairy miniboss spawns at 15 second mark, firing red triangle bullet pattern towards player
Fairy attacks for 5 seconds, then leaves the screen
Second fairy miniboss spawns after 20 seconds, firing red triangle bullet pattern towards player
Grunts and fairy miniboss leave at the 25 second mark
2 lines of grunts form a horizontal line across the upper middle screen
Each grunt shoots a circle bullet towards the player, then exit the screen

Mid boss attack - 25 sec
Mid boss appears along with health bar
Yellow arc bullet spray with three arcs, last arc is red bullets, originating from mid boss
Yellow circle bullet spray with three concentric circles, originating from mid boss, slightly faster pattern than the arc spray
Alternates between arc and circle spray of bullets for around twenty seconds
Mid boss leaves after twenty-five seconds is up, or once player defeats mid boss

More grunts - 45 sec
Two fairy minibosses spawn from each side of the screen, moving towards the opposite side of the screen, while attacking the player by shooting triangle shaped bullet patterns towards the player
Line of 5 grunts move to middle of the screen from left side and exit
Line of 5 grunts move to middle of the screen from right side and exit
After 5 seconds of fairy miniboss attacks, fairies exit
2 lines of grunts form a horizontal line across the upper middle screen
Each grunt shoots a circle bullet towards the player, then exit the screen
Two fairy minibosses spawn from each side of the screen, moving towards the opposite side of the screen, while attacking the player by shooting triangle shaped bullet patterns towards the player
Line of 5 grunts move to middle of the screen from left side and exit
Line of 5 grunts move to middle of the screen from right side and exit
After 5 seconds of fairy miniboss attacks, fairies exit
2 lines of grunts form a horizontal line across the upper middle screen
Each grunt shoots a circle bullet towards the player, then exit the screen
6 grunts form a pyramid shape on the left side of screen, and fire bullets toward the player, while moving toward the right side of screen.
6 grunts form a pyramid shape on the right side of screen, and fire bullets toward the player, while moving toward the left side of screen.
Two fairy minibosses spawn from each side of the screen, moving towards the opposite side of the screen, while attacking the player by shooting triangle shaped bullet patterns towards the player
Line of 5 grunts move to middle of the screen from left side and exit
Line of 5 grunts move to middle of the screen from right side and exit
After 5 seconds of fairy miniboss attacks, fairies exit

Final Boss - 90 sec
First stage of attack - 30 seconds
One red double helix bullet shape, originating from boss, follows the player
One blue circle bullet shape spinning left, originating from boss, getting consistently larger
One blue circle bullet shape spinning right, originating from boss, getting consistently larger
Second stage of attack ~ 30 seconds
One blue circle of bullets spinning left and one red circle of bullets spinning right originating from the boss, gets consistently larger in bursts, and alternates directions of bullets with each burst
One blue circle of bullets spinning right and one red circle of bullets spinning left originating from the boss, gets consistently larger in bursts, and alternates directions of bullets with each burst
Alternates between two above bullet patterns
Between bullets spawning, the boss moves randomly around the top third of the screen.
Boss finishes this attack once player gets the boss down to 75 Hp
Third stage of attack ~ 20 seconds
One blue circle spinning left that pulsates, originating from the boss, gets consistently larger
One blue circle spinning right that pulsates, originating from boss, gets consistently larger
Three red spirals, originating from same spot, bottom spiral follows player
These attacks repeat as boss moves randomly across top third of the screen
Once boss is defeated, boss falls down the screen

Game stats are displayed on the screen with buttons to take player to the home screen or play again
